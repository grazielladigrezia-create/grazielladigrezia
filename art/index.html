ARTISTIC AND THIRD-MISSION CURRICULUM

Profile at a glance

Profile
Poet and classical pianist (stage name Grazyella); creator and curator of the Mail Poetry Project®, a registered trademark; radiologist and university researcher
Artistic qualifications
Second-level Academic Degree in Ensemble Music, 110/110 (2025), and Diploma in Piano, 10/10 cum laude (2004), State Conservatory of Music "D. Cimarosa", Avellino, Italy
Poetry
Seven published collections, from Anima (2000) to L'attesa (2023); juror for poetry prizes; Regional Coordinator for Campania of Rinascimento Poetico (since 2024)
Mail Poetry Project®
Since 2023, bilingual international open calls, free of charge and without a jury, leading to physical and digital exhibitions on social themes: some 400 postcards from 20 countries in the first exhibition (2023); 153 works from 8 countries in TAILOR-MED (2026, with Çankırı Karatekin University, Türkiye); about one hundred artists from 15 countries in the 4th edition of "Stop Violence on Women" (2026). Venues in Avellino, Naples, Prato, Bari, Stradella and online, with the Museo Storico della Comunicazione, the Tavolo dei Postali and Poste Italiane
Music
Chamber pianist and piano four-hands performer; since 2024, Graziella & sons, a family ensemble with her three children; "musical postcards", a format pairing exhibitions of poetic postcards with live music
Scientific activity
Tenure-track Researcher in Diagnostic Imaging (MEDS-22/A), Link Campus University, Rome; National Scientific Qualification as Full and Associate Professor; PhD. Research on advanced breast imaging, artificial intelligence and personalised prevention. Expert Pool, ECIBC, European Commission; International Advisory Board, Canadian Association of Radiologists Journal; Guest Editor of 13 special issues; Editorial Board of 9 international journals. Profiles: Link Campus University · ORCID · Scopus
Art and medicine
Third Mission Lead for the Bachelor's degree in Medical Radiography (Link Campus University, since 2025); exhibition on breast cancer prevention at the National Congress of the SIRM Breast Imaging Section (Bari, 2025); two articles on dual careers in medicine and the arts (2026)
Associations
Board member, Università Irpina del Tempo Libero (since 2000); founding member and board member, Lupus in Fabula 2.0 (since 2025); President, IAAH (since 2026); registered freelance journalist (since 2024)

Mail Poetry Project®: international calls and exhibitions

Conceived in 2023 and registered as a trademark in 2024, the project is an offshoot of mail art that brings visual art and poetry together on a single postcard. Each edition opens with a bilingual (Italian and English) international call, open to all, with no entry fee and no jury; every work received is exhibited and documented.
