Graziella Di Grezia, MD, PhD

Tenure-track Researcher (Assistant Professor) in Diagnostic Imaging · Link Campus University, Rome, Italy

g.digrezia@unilink.it · Link Campus University profile
ORCID 0000-0003-4507-3505 · Scopus Author ID 36472945200 · WoS ResearcherID MCK-2924-2025

Profile

Breast radiologist, board-certified since 2012, with scientific responsibility for first- and second-level radiology in the organised mammography screening programme of ASL Avellino until 2024. Research focuses on contrast-enhanced mammography, breast MRI, automated breast ultrasound and AI-based risk stratification for personalised screening. National Scientific Qualification as Full Professor (2026).
